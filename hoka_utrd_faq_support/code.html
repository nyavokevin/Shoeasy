thumb_up RECOMMENDED RECOVERY CYCLE

  • 1. Dry first, brush second: Allow heavy mud cakes to dry naturally at room temp. Once dry, bang out dried clods and brush away clay with a soft horsehair or nylon brush.
  • 2. Lukewarm bath: Use a sponge with mild eco-friendly dish soap and lukewarm water (<30°C). Clean the hydrophobic mesh upper without submerging entirely.
  • 3. Air Dry with newspaper: Remove the Ortholite® insole and stuff shoes with recycled paper. Dry in a ventilated shaded corridor.

cancel CRITICAL WARNINGS

NEVER machine wash or tumble dry: Machine agitators fracture the supercritical nitrogen-infused foam lattice and weaken midsole heat-welds. Excessive heat also delaminates Vibram® Megagrip outsole lugs and dissolves structural overlays.

Avoid direct sunlight exposure or resting shoes directly on home radiator heaters.

Heel-to-toe drop is the elevation differential between where your heel rests and where the ball of your foot rests inside the shoe chassis.

UTRD shoes deliberately utilize a low-drop profile (typically 4mm to 5mm) paired with our patented Meta-Rocker Geometry. This engineering serves two primary biomechanical goals:

  • Natural Midfoot Strike: Low drop keeps the Achilles tendon active and engages calf musculature, naturally guiding your landing underneath your center of mass rather than over-striding on your heel.
  • Ascent & Alpine Agility: When scaling 25%+ steep gradients, low drop enables full ankle dorsiflexion, preventing premature calf fatigue and allowing maximum lug engagement against loose gravel.
